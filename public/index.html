Research Areas

We combine computation with biology to understand how cells adapt, fail, and evolve.

Oxidative Stress Biology

How cells sense and respond to reactive oxygen species. We model redox networks, protein oxidation, and stress-response regulation in bacteria and yeast.

Antimicrobial Resistance Evolution

Using population genomics and phylogenomics to trace how resistance mutations spread across clinical and environmental microbiomes.

Multi-omics Integration

Connecting transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics to build mechanistic models of stress response with reproducible, open pipelines.

Molecular Dynamics Simulations

All-atom and coarse-grained MD to understand how oxidative modifications alter protein structure, stability, and interaction networks.
