BM Lab is officially up and running at VIT Vellore. The lab focuses on computational approaches to oxidative stress biology, antimicrobial resistance evolution, and multi-omics integration.

We are recruiting PhD students and project interns for 2025. If you are interested in joining, see the [Join Us](/join/) page for details on what we look for and how to apply.

More updates on ongoing projects and collaborations will follow.
